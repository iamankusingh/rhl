Our Journey to the Top.

Rudra Heavy Lifters was founded in Anjar with a singular vision: to provide unparalleled lifting and logistics solutions to the rapidly expanding industrial sectors of Gujarat.

What started as a modest fleet has grown into an extensive arsenal of state-of-the-art mobile cranes, hydra trucks, and heavy transport vehicles. Today, we are proud to be the trusted partners for leading names across the industrial landscape.

Our growth is a testament to our unwavering commitment to safety, continuous technological upgrades, and the sheer dedication of our certified operators who ensure every lift goes exactly as planned.
